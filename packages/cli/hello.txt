i am nirvan jha from iiitdm kancheepuram !Good eating habits are essential for maintaining overall health and well-being. Start by eating a balanced diet rich in fruits, vegetables, whole grains, and lean proteins. Stay hydrated by drinking plenty of water throughout the day, and try to limit processed foods, excessive sugar, and unhealthy fats. Eating regular, mindful meals rather than skipping them helps maintain steady energy levels and supports proper digestion. Portion control and chewing food slowly can also improve digestion and prevent overeating. Finally, listen to your body's hunger and fullness cues to develop a healthy, sustainable relationship with food.
